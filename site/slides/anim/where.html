One core: a plain loop

One core, vectorized: NumPy runs a compiled loop over the whole array, and the core's vector units handle several numbers per instruction. Vectorize first; it's often the biggest win.

One chip: multiprocessing, concurrent.futures, joblib

One computer: a GPU splits the data across thousands of small cores: PyTorch, CuPy, Numba

Many computers: a cluster such as NC State's Hazel. Job arrays, Dask, Ray, mpi4py
